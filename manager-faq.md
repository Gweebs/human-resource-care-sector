# Manager FAQ

## When should I start with a welfare meeting?
Start there when the issue looks like an early, supportable problem rather than serious misconduct.

## What is a matter of concern?
It is an issue that may need fact-finding before deciding whether formal discipline is appropriate.

## Can I skip straight to a final warning?
Only if the seriousness and policy/legal framework justify it. Do not leapfrog fairness.

## Should I suspend automatically?
No. Suspension is a neutral step, not a default punishment.

## What if the concern is in a care home or care service?
You should consider safeguarding, CQC reporting duties, and whether the issue affects safe care.

## What if the employee is an agency worker or bank staff?
Check the contract and the worker arrangement first. The process may differ, but fairness still matters.

## Do I need to offer appeal?
Yes, as part of good practice and UK guidance.
