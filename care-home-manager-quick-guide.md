# Care-home manager quick guide

## 1. Spot the issue
Ask:
- Is this a welfare issue, conduct issue, capability issue, or safeguarding issue?

## 2. Start proportionately
- minor issue → welfare meeting, supervision, coaching
- unclear issue → fact-finding
- safety issue → safeguarding escalation and prompt investigation

Supervision should be regular and recorded. Use it to discuss performance, values, behaviour, wellbeing, safeguarding, conduct, support needs, and agreed actions.

## 3. Keep records accurate
- write down facts, not assumptions
- keep care notes, handover records, incident reports, and supervision notes together
- record low-level concerns early, especially where there is a repeated pattern

## 4. Use the right route
- capability for performance/training issues
- disciplinary for conduct/misconduct
- whistleblowing for protected disclosures

## 5. Escalate fairly
- written notice
- investigation
- meeting
- outcome letter
- appeal

## 6. In care homes, remember
- resident safety comes first
- medication errors may require immediate review
- falsified records are serious
- alcohol or drug impairment at work must be addressed quickly
- Skills for Care guidance supports at least monthly supervision for staff, with more supervision where risk has been identified
