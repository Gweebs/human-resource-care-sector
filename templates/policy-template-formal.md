# Formal disciplinary policy template

## 1. Policy statement
The organisation is committed to managing conduct and performance issues fairly, consistently, and promptly, in accordance with applicable UK law and the Acas Code of Practice.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees and, where contractually appropriate, to workers, agency staff, and bank staff.

## 3. Principles
The organisation will:
- act fairly and consistently
- investigate matters promptly and impartially
- provide employees with sufficient information about concerns
- allow employees a reasonable opportunity to respond
- allow accompaniment where required by law or policy
- consider welfare, support, and reasonable adjustments where relevant
- keep accurate and secure records
- offer an appeal against formal outcomes

## 4. Informal resolution
Minor or first-time concerns may be addressed through welfare meetings, supervision, coaching, training, or informal management action.

## 5. Formal disciplinary procedure
Where informal action is insufficient, the matter may proceed to:
- written notification of the concern
- investigation
- disciplinary hearing
- outcome
- appeal

## 6. Sanctions
Potential sanctions include:
- no formal action
- first written warning
- final written warning
- dismissal, including summary dismissal in cases of gross misconduct where justified

## 7. Investigation
The investigation will be proportionate to the nature and seriousness of the issue and will be carried out by an appropriate person who has not predetermmed the outcome.

## 8. Hearing
The employee will be informed of the allegations and evidence and given a reasonable opportunity to respond before any decision is taken.

## 9. Appeal
An appeal may be lodged against a formal outcome within the timescale set out in the organisation’s procedures.

## 10. Record keeping
All stages of the process, including notes, evidence, correspondence, and outcomes, will be documented and retained securely.
