# Care-industry examples

## Example 1: Repeated lateness for a care assistant
- Start with a welfare meeting if the issue appears minor and supportable.
- Agree review dates and expectations.
- Escalate to formal warning if lateness continues.

## Example 2: Missed documentation in a care home
- Treat as a matter of concern if records are incomplete but there is no immediate harm.
- Investigate whether it is training, workload, or conduct.
- Consider a first warning or support plan.

## Example 3: Safeguarding lapse
- If a staff member failed to follow safeguarding procedure, assess seriousness immediately.
- Consider suspension only if necessary to protect residents or preserve evidence.
- Follow formal investigation and disciplinary meeting.
- If the breach is serious, final warning or dismissal may be appropriate depending on facts and policy.

## Example 4: Medication error
- Distinguish between human error, training need, and negligence.
- Check incident reports, supervision records, and competence.
- Consider CQC reporting obligations and whether the issue affects safe service delivery.
