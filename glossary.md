# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Welfare meeting | Early supportive meeting to address concerns informally |
| Matters of concern | Issues that may require fact-finding or escalation |
| Supervision | A regular recorded meeting or discussion between a manager and staff member to review work, support needs, standards, performance, wellbeing, learning, conduct, and safeguarding concerns. It can identify and record concerns, but it is not a disciplinary sanction. |
| Investigation | Evidence-gathering stage before formal hearing |
| Disciplinary meeting | Formal meeting where allegations and evidence are considered |
| Written warning | Formal warning that conduct/performance must improve |
| Final warning | Last formal warning before dismissal if issues persist |
| Dismissal | Termination of employment |
| Appeal | Review of the decision by someone appropriate and impartial |
