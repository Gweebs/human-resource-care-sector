# Sources And Boundaries Reference

Use this when stating evidence limits, caveats, source hierarchy, or legal boundaries.

## Source Hierarchy

Use sources in this order:

1. User-provided staff handbook, contract, policy, or case facts
2. Project disciplinary process materials
3. Acas Code of Practice and Acas guide
4. GOV.UK disciplinary and dismissal guidance
5. CQC safeguarding or regulatory context where care quality or reporting is involved
6. Skills for Care guidance on supervision, safer employment, people management, and identifying concerns
7. Legal advice where dismissal, safeguarding, protected characteristics, whistleblowing, or complex contract issues are involved

## Legal Boundary

Use wording like:

"This is HR process and drafting support, not legal advice. Before dismissal, summary dismissal, safeguarding referral, or action involving protected characteristics, whistleblowing, sickness, disability, contract status, or complex evidence, check current Acas, GOV.UK, internal policy, contract wording, safeguarding duties, and legal advice where needed."

## When Facts Are Missing

Say:
- what is known
- what is not known
- what needs checking
- the next step

Example:

"What is known: [facts]. What is not known: [missing evidence]. Next step: gather [records/witness account/supervision note] before deciding whether formal action is proportionate."

## Weak Evidence

If evidence is weak, say so clearly.

Use:

"The evidence does not yet support a formal finding. The next step is fact-finding, not an outcome decision."

or:

"There may be a concern, but the current record does not show whether this is conduct, capability, welfare, or a training issue."

## Risk Flags

Flag these:
- dismissal or summary dismissal
- gross misconduct
- safeguarding risk
- medication error or unsafe care
- falsified records
- whistleblowing
- grievance linked to discipline
- sickness, disability, pregnancy, race, religion, sex, age, or other protected characteristic
- agency, bank, contractor, or probationary status
- lack of evidence
- delay in process
- predetermination
- inconsistent treatment
- suspension without clear reason

## Acas Fairness Checks

Check:
- clear allegation
- prompt and fair investigation
- chance to respond
- evidence shared where appropriate
- right to be accompanied where applicable
- proportionate outcome
- written decision
- appeal route
