# Handbook Alignment Reference

Use this when the user asks for wording that mimics or aligns with a staff handbook.

## Handbook Areas To Reflect

The project source pack expects handbook alignment around:
- disciplinary procedures
- capability procedures
- appeal rights
- grievance procedure
- whistleblowing
- termination of employment
- gross misconduct examples
- alcohol and drugs affecting attendance or performance
- falsifying records
- unauthorised removal of property or data

## Tone Difference

Use the handbook as the local policy source.

Use the disciplinary process material as the fairness and best-practice guide.

Handbook tone is usually operational and employer-facing. It sets rules and consequences.

Process guidance should be fairness-led and evidence-led.

## Route Separation

Keep these routes separate:

| Route | Use for |
|---|---|
| Disciplinary | Conduct, misconduct, rule breaches, dishonesty, unsafe choices |
| Capability | Skills, competence, training, performance ability, genuine inability |
| Welfare | Health, stress, personal difficulty, reasonable support |
| Grievance | Employee complaint about treatment or workplace issue |
| Whistleblowing | Protected disclosure or concern raised about wrongdoing |
| Safeguarding | Resident risk, abuse, neglect, dignity, safety, unsafe care |

## Serious Or Gross Misconduct Examples

Treat these as potentially serious in care settings:
- falsifying care records
- serious medication breach
- safeguarding breach
- abuse, neglect, or poor treatment of a resident
- attending work impaired by alcohol or drugs
- unauthorised removal of records, property, or data
- serious dishonesty
- serious breach of confidentiality
- violence, threat, or serious misconduct towards residents, staff, or visitors

Gross misconduct may justify summary dismissal only after a fair process unless there is a lawful reason why that cannot happen.

## Handbook-Aligned Wording

Use wording like:

"The organisation may treat falsification of records, unauthorised removal of records or property, serious breach of confidentiality, safeguarding breaches, or attending work impaired by alcohol or drugs as serious misconduct. Each case will be considered on its facts and handled through a fair process."

For capability:

"Where the concern appears to relate to skill, training, competence, health, or genuine ability to meet the required standard, the matter should be considered under the capability or welfare route before disciplinary action is considered."

For whistleblowing:

"A concern raised as a protected disclosure must not be treated as misconduct because the employee raised it. Any separate conduct issue must be considered separately and carefully."
