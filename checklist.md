# Manager checklist

## Before formal action
- [ ] Is the issue factual and documented?
- [ ] Has welfare/support been considered?
- [ ] Is there enough evidence to proceed?
- [ ] Is the issue misconduct, performance, capability, or conduct-related?

## During process
- [ ] Has the employee been told the allegation clearly?
- [ ] Have they been given a chance to respond?
- [ ] Has the evidence been shared appropriately?
- [ ] Has accompaniment been offered where applicable?
- [ ] Are notes being kept?

## Outcomes
- [ ] Is the outcome proportionate?
- [ ] Does the warning set clear expectations and a review date?
- [ ] Is the appeal route explained?
- [ ] Are notice and contractual obligations handled?

## Care-sector checks
- [ ] Safeguarding implications considered
- [ ] Resident safety and dignity considered
- [ ] Reporting duties checked where applicable
