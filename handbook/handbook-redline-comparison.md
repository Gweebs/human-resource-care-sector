# Redline comparison — staff handbook vs disciplinary pack

## Handbook points already reflected in the pack
- disciplinary procedures → covered in `../process/process-map.md`, `../templates/policy-template-formal.md`, and `../process/master-brief.md`
- capability procedures → covered in `../templates/policy-template-formal.md`, `../process/decision-tree.md`, and `../process/master-brief.md`
- appeal rights → covered in `../process/process-map.md`, `../process/checklist.md`, `../process/manager-faq.md`, and `../templates/policy-template-formal.md`
- grievance procedure → noted in the UK framework and source index, but kept separate from disciplinary handling
- whistleblowing → captured as a separate concern that should not be confused with misconduct retaliation
- termination of employment → covered as a fair outcome at the end of the process
- gross misconduct examples → reflected in dismissal/final warning guidance and care-sector examples
- alcohol and drugs affecting attendance/performance → reflected in handbook integration notes and care-sector guidance
- falsifying records → reflected as potentially serious misconduct, especially in care records
- unauthorised removal of property/data → reflected as misconduct / serious conduct issue

## Differences in tone
- Handbook: operational and employer-facing, with direct rules and consequences
- Disciplinary pack: process-focused, fairness-led, and reference-oriented

## Care-home specific alignment
The handbook’s emphasis on records, attendance, conduct, and alcohol/drugs at work maps well to care settings where:
- handover accuracy matters
- resident notes must be reliable
- medication records must be correct
- safeguarding duties may require immediate escalation

## Suggested use
Use the handbook as the local policy source and the disciplinary pack as the process and best-practice guide.
