# Handbook-based case examples

## Example 1: Falsified care records
A care worker completes notes late and then backfills details inaccurately.

Likely route:
- investigation
- disciplinary meeting
- serious misconduct consideration

Why it matters:
- falsifying records can affect resident safety, legal compliance, and audit integrity

## Example 2: Alcohol-related impairment at work
A staff member attends shift appearing impaired, affecting attendance and task performance.

Likely route:
- immediate welfare/safety response
- fact-finding
- formal disciplinary route if appropriate

Why it matters:
- the handbook-style rule links alcohol/drug issues to attendance, safety, and performance

## Example 3: Unauthorised removal of records or property
An employee takes paperwork or equipment home without permission.

Likely route:
- fact-finding
- disciplinary meeting
- possible serious misconduct

Why it matters:
- unauthorised removal of property/data can justify serious action depending on facts

## Example 4: Capability issue
A worker struggles with new record-keeping systems after a change in process.

Likely route:
- capability route
- training and support
- review period

Why it matters:
- capability should not be treated as misconduct if the issue is skills or performance rather than wrongdoing
