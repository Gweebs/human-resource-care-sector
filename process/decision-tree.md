# Decision tree

## A. Is there a concern?
- No → no disciplinary action
- Yes → go to B

## B. Is it minor, isolated, and suitable for support?
- Yes → welfare meeting / coaching / supervision
- No → go to C

## C. Is there enough information to understand the issue?
- No → fact-finding / investigation
- Yes → go to D

## D. Is the issue misconduct, capability, or serious breach?
- Misconduct/capability → formal disciplinary route
- Serious breach / gross misconduct → consider suspension and immediate investigation

## E. Is formal action proportionate?
- No → informal management action or support plan
- Yes → proceed to meeting and outcome

## F. Has the employee responded and been heard?
- No → do not finalise
- Yes → decide outcome

## G. Outcome
- no action
- first written warning
- final written warning
- dismissal / termination (only if justified and fair)
