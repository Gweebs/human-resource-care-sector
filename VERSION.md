# Version 1.1

Released: 25 June 2026

## Added

- Welfare meeting record template.
- Skill guidance for drafting and reviewing welfare meeting records.
- Care-sector manager checks for welfare, equality, reasonable adjustments, occupational health, work-related causes, and safe care impact.

## Notes

Welfare meeting records are supportive HR records. They should not be treated as disciplinary sanctions. Move to fact-finding, safeguarding, grievance, whistleblowing, capability, or conduct routes only where the facts support that step.
