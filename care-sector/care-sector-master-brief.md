# Care-sector master brief — disciplinary process

## Purpose
A care-sector-specific disciplinary reference for regulated services, focused on fair process, safeguarding, resident safety, supervision, and evidence handling.

## Executive summary
In care settings, disciplinary issues should be handled through a fair and consistent process that balances employee welfare with resident safety. Early concerns may be managed informally through welfare meetings, supervision, coaching, and support. Where the issue is more serious or persists, the employer should move into fact-finding, investigation, and a formal disciplinary meeting.

Care-sector cases often need extra attention to safeguarding, medication handling, documentation, competence, and reporting duties. The disciplinary process should be proportionate, evidence-based, and aligned with Acas best practice, CQC duties, and Skills for Care guidance on identifying and managing concerns.

Skills for Care guidance should be used as a sector source for supervision, people performance management, safer employment, and identifying concerns. It supports regular supervision, early recording of low-level concerns, manager training, and a safeguarding mindset when conduct or behaviour may affect safe care.

## Typical care-sector flow
1. Welfare meeting / informal support
2. Matters of concern / fact-finding
3. Investigation
4. Formal disciplinary meeting
5. Outcome: no action, warning, final warning, or dismissal
6. Appeal
7. Closure and any safeguarding / regulatory follow-up

## Key care-sector considerations
- safeguarding of service users
- medication errors and record accuracy
- dignity and respectful conduct
- regular supervision, competence checks, and evidence of support
- reporting duties to the regulator where required
- whether suspension is necessary to protect people or evidence
- early recording of low-level concerns or repeated patterns
- manager training in supervision, investigation, and safeguarding-minded decision making

## Best-practice sources
- Acas Code of Practice and guide
- GOV.UK disciplinary and dismissal guidance
- CQC safeguarding and misconduct-related guidance
- Skills for Care guidance on identifying and managing concerns, supervision, and safe and fair practice
