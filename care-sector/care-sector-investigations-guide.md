# Care-sector investigations and evidence guide

## Purpose
To support fair investigations in care settings where resident safety, safeguarding, or staff conduct is in question.

## Evidence sources
- incident reports
- care notes and daily records
- medication administration records
- supervision records
- training and competency records
- witness statements
- CCTV where lawful and relevant
- safeguarding logs
- shift rotas and staffing records

## Good practice
- appoint an impartial investigator
- preserve evidence promptly
- avoid leading questions
- interview relevant staff separately where appropriate
- distinguish fact from opinion
- document the chain of reasoning
- review supervision notes and one-to-one records for earlier concerns, support offered, standards set, and agreed actions
- check whether low-level concerns were shared, recorded, and escalated at the right time

## Care-sector specific questions
- Did the issue affect resident safety or dignity?
- Was there a safeguarding implication?
- Did supervision or training identify the risk earlier?
- Was the staff member competent for the task?
- Was the incident report and escalation handled correctly?
- Were there repeated conduct, behaviour, or performance concerns before the incident?
