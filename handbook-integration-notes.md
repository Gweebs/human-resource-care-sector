# Staff handbook integration notes

## Source handbook observations
The handbook includes formal sections on:
- disciplinary procedures
- capability procedures
- appeal rights
- grievance procedure
- whistleblowing
- termination of employment
- gross misconduct examples
- alcohol and drugs affecting attendance/performance
- falsifying records
- unauthorised removal of property/data

## Integration points for the disciplinary pack
- reinforce that poor record keeping and falsifying records can be treated as serious misconduct
- include capability/performance as a distinct route from misconduct
- emphasise appeal rights and hearing fairness
- link alcohol/drug issues to attendance, safety, and performance concerns
- note that whistleblowing concerns should be handled separately from retaliation or misconduct cases
- note that termination and dismissal are distinct outputs of a fair process, not separate shortcuts

## Care-sector relevance
In a care setting, the handbook-style rules matter especially for:
- medication records
- resident notes
- safeguarding logs
- handover accuracy
- alcohol or drug-related impairment at work
- removal of records or property
