# Care-sector manager training notes

## Core learning points
- use informal support early where appropriate
- know when concerns become formal
- investigate fairly and promptly
- understand safeguarding escalation
- record accurately and factually
- consider resident safety before deciding suspension or escalation
- keep warning letters specific, time-bound, and proportionate
- provide appeal routes and document outcomes

## Scenario prompts
- repeated lateness affecting handover
- medication documentation error
- unexplained bruise or safeguarding concern
- poor note-keeping
- rude or dismissive conduct toward a resident
